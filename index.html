About

I'm a graphic designer based in Toronto with two years of in-house experience. As a junior graphic designer at a commercial lighting manufacturer, I produced product catalogues, social media content, web graphics and product photography. I also freelance for nonprofits and small businesses.

I work across advertising campaigns, brand identity, packaging and editorial layout, and I'm happiest when a single idea has to hold up from a social post to a billboard.

I've designed for nonprofits and small businesses in Georgia and Canada, from a youth empowerment organization to a Georgian bakery in Toronto. I design in both English and Georgian, so bilingual typography is a regular part of my work.
